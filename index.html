About Us

Trusted Care, Powered by MediVance

MediVance is a doctor-patient management platform connecting patients with a trusted network of hospitals and specialists. Our mission is to make quality healthcare simple, transparent, and accessible — from booking an appointment to consulting your doctor, managing prescriptions, and tracking your health records, all in one place.

We partner with accredited hospitals and certified doctors across specialties including cardiology, pediatrics, orthopedics, and general medicine, ensuring every patient receives timely, reliable, and compassionate care.
